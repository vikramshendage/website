Pad Mounted

Compact, tamper-proof distribution transformers designed for safe and efficient power delivery in urban and underground networks. Ideal for residential, commercial, and industrial power distribution with minimal space requirements.

  • Capacity range: 25 kVA to 10 MVA
  • Primary voltage: Up to 44 kV
  • Secondary voltage: Up to 13.8 kV

Station Transformer

Reliable station type transformers designed to supply auxiliary and service power in substations. Ideal for operating control panels, protection systems, lighting, and cooling equipment, ensuring safe and continuous substation operation.

  • Capacity range: 25 kVA to 10 MVA
  • Cooling: ONAN / ANAF

Comprehensive Testing Program

Each station transformer manufactured by our company undergoes a structured factory testing program to verify electrical performance, design integrity, and workmanship. All tests are carried out in accordance with applicable IEEE / ANSI standards and project-specific requirements to ensure safe, reliable, and long-term operation.

Routine Tests as per IEEE C57.12.90
Insulation Resistance & Winding Resistance Tests
Impulse Tests (BIL/BSL)
Temperature Rise Tests
Short Circuit Tests
Dissolved Gas Analysis (DGA)
Ratio, Polarity & Phase Displacement Tests

All tests are performed at the manufacturing facility or at approved test laboratories, as applicable.
