Station Transformers

High-voltage power station transformers designed to IEEE/ANSI standards for utility substations and power generation facilities

Critical Substation Support Infrastructure

Station transformers play a vital role in substations by supplying auxiliary and service power required for control, protection, lighting, cooling, and monitoring systems. Designed for continuous and reliable operation, they ensure uninterrupted functionality of critical substation equipment.

Our station transformers are engineered for utility and industrial substations, featuring robust construction, dependable insulation systems, and proven cooling designs. Built to meet IEEE and CSA utility requirements, they deliver stable performance, high reliability, and long service life under demanding operating conditions.

Designed as per IEEE C57 / CSA C88
Tested in accordance with IEEE C57.12.90
Designed for continuous auxiliary duty
High short-circuit withstand capability
Reliable operation under varying load conditions
Suitable for indoor and outdoor substations

Station Transformer Types

Specialized transformers for different utility and industrial applications

Auxiliary / Station Service Transformers

Station service transformers supply auxiliary power required for substation operation.

  • Feeds control, protection, lighting & HVAC systems
  • Designed for continuous auxiliary duty
  • Indoor and outdoor installation
  • High reliability for critical substation loads

Control Transformers

Control transformers provide stable low-voltage power for control and automation circuits.

  • Used in relay, protection & automation panels
  • High accuracy and voltage stability
  • Low inrush current design
  • Compact and panel-mounted options

Grounding (Earthing) Transformers

Grounding transformers provide a neutral point for ungrounded or delta systems.

  • Improves system protection and fault detection
  • Zig-zag or wye-delta configurations
  • Used in substations and industrial systems
  • Limits ground fault current

Multiple-Secondary Station Transformers

Multi-secondary transformers supply separate auxiliary circuits from a single unit.

  • Independent supplies for critical & non-critical loads
  • Improves operational reliability
  • Reduces need for multiple transformers
  • Custom secondary voltages available
